# Vision

House Calendar replaces the paper calendar on the kitchen wall. An Android tablet hangs where the calendar used to and shows the family's week at a glance: a column for each person and a row for each day, as on a paper family organiser. Parents carry the same calendar on their phones. Anyone in the kitchen can see what is on today, where everyone needs to be, and who is taking them. A parent at the school gate can add "Ada, swimming, Tuesdays at 5, not in the holidays" in a few seconds, and it is on the kitchen wall by the time they get home. Every device keeps the whole calendar itself and they sync with each other directly over the home Wi-Fi, with no account, server or internet connection. So the calendar keeps working when the internet is down, and the family's movements are never stored by anyone else. Any family can set it up with the devices they already own. Version 1 covers events and activities. Its data model leaves room to add tasks, meal plans and shopping lists later without breaking anything already recorded. **Done means:** a family runs its real schedule on a kitchen tablet and two parents' phones for a full school half-term without the paper calendar or an internet connection. During that time every device shows the same calendar after each sync. Every recurring activity follows the term-time and daylight-saving rules without anyone correcting it. No event goes without a named person to take the child, unless the app is showing that it still needs one. Reminders fire on the phone of the person who needs them.
